## Findings ledger

Severity reflects potential impact, not proof of exploitation. Confidence describes the quoted observation; consequences marked conditional remain unverified.

- **FG-1 — Medium, confirmed static, high confidence.** [CI:157-163](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/forge/blob/c86e2381174baf2565c1dc2f791661f9cbee18b1/.github/workflows/ci.yml#L157-L163) requires `"${HARD:-0}" -ne 42` to be false, yet states “41 hard / 10 soft.” A maintainer cannot infer the intended contract from the messages. Derive the classification from harness source and reconcile without lowering requirements.
- **FG-2 — Medium, confirmed static, high confidence.** [CI:53-61](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/forge/blob/c86e2381174baf2565c1dc2f791661f9cbee18b1/.github/workflows/ci.yml#L53-L61) uses `set +e`, runs `cargo run -p golden-harness 2>&1 | tee harness.log`, then gates on parsed `PASS`. A nonzero process status is not explicitly checked; a qualifying summary could mask a later failure. Preserve status and validate complete output. No such masking was observed.
- **FG-3 — Medium, historical CI, high confidence in recorded conclusion only.** Snapshot reports failures for this exact pinned SHA in [41645248860](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/forge/actions/runs/41645248860), [41464067666](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/forge/actions/runs/41464067666), and [41307302684](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/forge/actions/runs/41307302684). These are historical results, not a current rerun; job logs and causes were not supplied. Do not attribute them to FG-1 or FG-2 without logs.
- **FG-4 — Medium, confirmed static / owner decision, high confidence in configuration.** [Release:5-7,18-25](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/forge/blob/c86e2381174baf2565c1dc2f791661f9cbee18b1/.github/workflows/release.yml#L5-L25) defaults `sign` and `notarize` to `false` and conditionally requires verification. Unsigned internal artifacts may be intentional; public distribution policy and actual releases are unknown. Separate internal and public release eligibility.
- **FG-5 — High, confirmed static, high confidence in conditional behavior.** On non-macOS, [main:33-36](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/forge/blob/c86e2381174baf2565c1dc2f791661f9cbee18b1/crates/aether-daemon/src/main.rs#L33-L36) sets a missing token to `unwrap_or_default()`; [server:11-16,99-108](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/forge/blob/c86e2381174baf2565c1dc2f791661f9cbee18b1/crates/aether-daemon/src/server.rs#L11-L16) returns `true` when `expected.is_empty()` and applies the check to non-ping requests. Therefore this configuration disables IPC authentication, including for task and workspace-grant methods. Reachability, real deployment and downstream execution were not tested. Define supported platforms and fail closed where authentication is required.
- **FG-6 — Medium, confirmed static / authorization risk, high confidence in observed label.** [Server:135-145,305-356](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/forge/blob/c86e2381174baf2565c1dc2f791661f9cbee18b1/crates/aether-daemon/src/server.rs#L305-L356) exposes `grant_workspace`, inserts `read` and `write` grants, and writes `"source": "authenticated_ui"`. The sampled handler establishes no separate UI-origin proof beyond the shared IPC gate; an authenticated non-UI client could receive that audit label. Whether shared-token clients are trusted to grant is an owner decision. Verify authorization and accurately label provenance.
- **FG-7 — Medium, confirmed static, high confidence in order dependence.** [MCP runtime:163-193](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/forge/blob/c86e2381174baf2565c1dc2f791661f9cbee18b1/crates/aether-mcp/src/runtime.rs#L163-L193) updates `aggregate` for each server-returned tool before `tools.sort_by(...)`. Identical tool sets returned in different orders can produce different hashes, and [lines 333-348](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/forge/blob/c86e2381174baf2565c1dc2f791661f9cbee18b1/crates/aether-mcp/src/runtime.rs#L333-L348) conditionally reject a mismatch. Determine whether order is intentionally part of the pin contract before changing it; compatibility and real impact are untested.

## Five-discipline assessment

Additional pinned anchors completing the ledger's multi-range citations: FG-5 applies its helper in the [request gate at server.rs:99-108](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/forge/blob/c86e2381174baf2565c1dc2f791661f9cbee18b1/crates/aether-daemon/src/server.rs#L99-L108); FG-6's method dispatch is at [server.rs:135-145](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/forge/blob/c86e2381174baf2565c1dc2f791661f9cbee18b1/crates/aether-daemon/src/server.rs#L135-L145). The automation assessment also relies on [ipc_auth.rs:119-152](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/forge/blob/c86e2381174baf2565c1dc2f791661f9cbee18b1/crates/aether-daemon/tests/ipc_auth.rs#L119-L152), in addition to the first test range linked below.

**Correctness/code review:** FG-5–7 concern actual sampled branches, not proof that a complete end-to-end exploit or pin failure occurred. IPC permits unauthenticated `ping`, while other methods use the gate ([server:99-108](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/forge/blob/c86e2381174baf2565c1dc2f791661f9cbee18b1/crates/aether-daemon/src/server.rs#L99-L108)); verify expected behavior and grant lifetimes.

**Automation/testing:** [IPC tests:36-67,119-152](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/forge/blob/c86e2381174baf2565c1dc2f791661f9cbee18b1/crates/aether-daemon/tests/ipc_auth.rs#L36-L67) contain wire-level checks for configured-token denial and rejected automation grants; they were not run and do not establish safe empty-token startup. CI's [PR-fast and non-PR full jobs](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/forge/blob/c86e2381174baf2565c1dc2f791661f9cbee18b1/.github/workflows/ci.yml#L64-L103) differ; harness totals alone do not establish individual security properties.

**DevOps/operations:** [CI:32-33](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/forge/blob/c86e2381174baf2565c1dc2f791661f9cbee18b1/.github/workflows/ci.yml#L32-L33) installs `@modelcontextprotocol/server-filesystem` without a version. This is a reproducibility and dependency-integrity risk, not evidence of compromise. Signatures, notarization, artifact provenance, update rollback and database restore remain unobserved.

**OWASP/security:** FG-5 and FG-6 require an explicit local-client threat model. The MCP path checks a grant and avoids including `extra_env` in its local audit payload ([runtime:298-304](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/forge/blob/c86e2381174baf2565c1dc2f791661f9cbee18b1/crates/aether-mcp/src/runtime.rs#L298-L304)); this does not prove secret redaction throughout logs or model/tool outputs. Injection containment, gateway authorization, credential-to-destination binding, encryption/retention and backup protection remain unverified. [Runtime:59-67](https://github.com/knarayanareddy/forge/blob/c86e2381174baf2565c1dc2f791661f9cbee18b1/crates/aether-mcp/src/runtime.rs#L59-L67) also exposes a direct spawn helper; whether production callers can bypass the sandbox is unknown.

**UI/UX/accessibility:** the inventory includes Swift desktop views, but their implementation was not inspected. Test visible consent, workspace selection, denial recovery, keyboard and VoiceOver behavior; no web frontend is assumed.

## Owner blockers, evidence and limitations

Owner decisions: supported platforms and local-client trust; intended grant provenance and hard/soft counts; approved model/runtime versions; access to historical logs and isolated runners; Developer ID, notarization and distribution accounts; retention, backup and restore targets. None is assumed available.

Inspected snapshot's complete sampled `README.md:1-261`, `Cargo.toml:1-13`, workflows `ci.yml:1-168` and `release.yml:1-34`, and eight crate manifests; plus pinned `aether-daemon/src/main.rs:1-66`, `server.rs:1-610`, `tests/ipc_auth.rs:1-153` and `aether-mcp/src/runtime.rs:1-382`. Tree inventory is not a source audit. Agent loop, Swift views, gateway handlers, sandbox internals, keychain, database recovery, film projects and production call sites were not inspected. No app execution, installation, test, signing, deploy or restore occurred. See [remediation plan](REMEDIATION_PLAN.md).
